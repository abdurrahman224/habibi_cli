GraceConnect Kids – Child Check-In & ReportingSafe

Fast children's ministry check-in with guardian matching and automatic reporting.

How It Works

  1. 1 A parent checks in at a kiosk, tablet, or in the app — the child's name pulls up in seconds.
  2. 2 The system prints a matching child label and guardian claim tag.
  3. 3 Only a matching tag can collect the child; volunteers see allergy and care flags instantly.
  4. 4 Every check-in flows into attendance and follow-up reports. Highlights: allergy & medical flags · room capacity limits · volunteer rosters · parent SMS alerts.

PeopleHub — Member & Visitor Journeys

One living profile for every person, from visitor to member to leader.

How It Works

  1. 1 A new face is captured (connect card, app signup, or check-in) and a profile is created.
  2. 2 Follow-up steps trigger automatically — a welcome message, a care-team assignment.
  3. 3 Staff see the full timeline: attendance, giving, groups, notes, and milestones.
  4. 4 Statuses move people along your assimilation pipeline: Visitor → Regular → Member → Serving. Highlights: household grouping · custom fields · care notes · assimilation pipeline · duplicate merge.

Community — Groups, Tags & Departments

Organize the whole church into ministries, departments, and dynamic tags.

How It Works

  1. 1 Create groups and departments, and give each leader their own view.
  2. 2 Tag people by any attribute (new believer, choir, first-aid trained).
  3. 3 Leaders manage their own rosters, attendance, and messaging.
  4. 4 Dynamic tags update themselves as people's data changes. Highlights: leader permissions · self-signup groups · group attendance · smart tags.

Presence – Attendance Tracking

Customizable attendance across services, groups, classes, and events.

How It Works

  1. 1 Choose how you count — headcount, individual check-in, or QR self-scan.
  2. 2 Capture from a kiosk, the app, or a leader's device.
  3. 3 Trends roll up into dashboards by service, group, and demographic.
  4. 4 Attendance drops raise follow-up flags for care teams. Highlights: custom attendance types · QR self check-in · absence alerts · historical trends.
